# music-bot

A page of music recommendations for Maike Osborne, built each morning on her Mac by her music recommender and published here through GitHub Pages: https://mosb.github.io/music-bot/

It lists new artists near what she has been playing, unowned albums she has played lately, her Bandcamp wishlist, and the unowned albums she has played most, as a list to buy from.
Tapping a cover plays the album from its first track where it is on Bandcamp, else opens its YouTube Music playlist.
The order of every list but the buy list is redrawn on each load.

Every build replaces the single commit on `main`; there is nothing here but the page, its covers and the manifest that lets Safari add it to a Home Screen.
