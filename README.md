# music-bot

A page of music recommendations for Maike Osborne, built each morning on her Mac by her music recommender and published here through GitHub Pages: https://mosb.github.io/music-bot/

It lists new artists near what she has been playing, unowned albums she has played lately, her Bandcamp wishlist, the albums in her own library that the recommender ranks highest, albums by artists she has no history with, and a daily draw of albums worth buying.
Tapping a cover opens Bandcamp's embedded player where the album is on Bandcamp, else its YouTube Music playlist.
The order of every list is redrawn on each load.

Every build replaces the single commit on `main`; there is nothing here but the page, its covers and the manifest that lets Safari add it to a Home Screen.
